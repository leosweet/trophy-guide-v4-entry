    Five stages from the first mission to the platinum. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 at about 200 hours: 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours of 100% checklist cleanup, and a final 10 hours of miscellaneous trophies. Work the stages in order — most trophies in a later stage need tools, characters or areas unlocked earlier.

    Only one trophy can be missed, and only in Chapter 1. Everything else stays available after the story in free-roam. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots) as insurance.
